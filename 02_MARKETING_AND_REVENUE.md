```
Monthly Revenue = (Monthly Sessions × Pages/Session × CTR × CPC)
             OR = (Monthly Pageviews × CPM / 1000)

Example Year 1, Month 12:
= 40,000 sessions × 3 pages × 3% CTR × $0.50 CPC
= 40,000 × 3 × 0.03 × 0.50
= $1,800/month (high estimate)

OR using CPM method:
= 120,000 pageviews × $5 CPM / 1000
= $600/month (conservative)
```
